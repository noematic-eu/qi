Cozyplanes is top-down air traffic, drawn with a finger. There is no money in the round: draw the route, land the plane, and the quota opens the next airport.

A plane enters from the edge of the map. You draw its path. It follows the line, then keeps its heading. It lands only if the end of the stroke aims at its runway, on axis. Two planes that touch end the shift.

Each landing scores one point. The pace rises with the score. An airport’s record is its best landing count.

<br>

> Five airports. One finger on each plane.

Mouse, finger, and a few keys are enough.

### Airports

1. **Pré-Vert.** A long runway, a short runway, and a helicopter pad.
2. **Grève.** Grass, sand, water. The seaplane lands on the pier.
3. **Quai.** Two runways crossing.
4. **Brise.** The wind turns. Only the into-wind end is open.
5. **La Tournée.** A courier follows a fixed route. You cannot divert it.

### Controls

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Draw or redraw a route | drag from the plane |
| Hook the stroke to the runway | release near the right end |
| Cancel the stroke | right click |
| Pause | Space |
| Shift speed | ×1 / ×2 |
| Back to the airports | Esc |
| Retry after a collision | R |
| Mute or unmute | M, or the sound button |
