### Tips

#### Approach from behind

Sheep flee the dog. Come from behind the flock to push them toward the pen instead of scattering them.

#### Gate discipline

Open the gate to let sheep in, then **close** it to finish. Leaving it open invites escapes.

#### Stars, not cruelty

There is no hard fail timer — go slow if you need to. Faster runs earn more stars.
