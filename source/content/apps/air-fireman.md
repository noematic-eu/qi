### Tips

#### Drop water against the wind

Plane speed and wind spray water over a larger area. For precision, fly and drop **into** the wind so water reaches the fire.

#### Fuel and lakes

Watch the gas gauge. Land on the runway to refuel; skim lakes to refill water before the tank runs dry.
