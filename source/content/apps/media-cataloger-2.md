Today, <a href="/media-cataloger">Media Cataloger 0.0.1</a> is a free download, limited to one disk.

### Features

The license file lets you add a disk and look for copies. Everything stays on your computer. [How this compares with other catalogs](/blog/compare-disk-catalogs).

#### Local and offline disk structure

Even disconnected, you can view the folder tree, modification dates, and sizes.

#### Instant disk overview

Free space at a glance.

#### Visualize disk space consumption

See which folders and files take up the most space, so you can decide what to keep.

#### File search

By name, by extension, by date, or by camera, across all catalogued drives — connected or not. The date and the camera are read while the disk is plugged in. Search still works once the disk is back in the drawer.

#### Duplicate file detection

Copies on the same disk or across several disks.

#### Customizable duplicate lists

By subject, owner, or any criteria you choose.

#### Unique file finder

Files that exist on only one disk.

#### Remove unnecessary duplicates

Free space by taking out redundant copies.

#### Large catalogs

A large catalog stays usable.

#### Cross-platform

Windows, macOS, and Linux. iOS and Android are not part of this offer.

### Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

#### Media Cataloger or Media Cataloger 2?

**One product line, two pages.** <a href="/media-cataloger">Media Cataloger</a> is the free download, limited to one disk, available now. Media Cataloger 2 is the download planned for **15 October 2026**. You are not buying two apps.

#### I already have a license?

Yes. The file we already emailed still works. Paste it once in the 15 October app. On Mac, the app that looks like a normal Mac app does not read it yet.

#### Does one file work on Mac, Windows, and Linux?

Yes, in the 15 October app. The free download stays on <a href="/media-cataloger">Media Cataloger</a>, limited to one disk.

#### I use a Mac. Which download reads the license?

The 15 October download. The other Mac app is still being finished and does not read the file yet. The free download is the older one, for Intel Macs, limited to one disk.

#### Is it open source?

Some parts. Media Cataloger 2 is a Noematic app. The free download is public.

#### Does it need the internet?

Not to catalog, search, or find duplicates. Everything stays on your machine.
