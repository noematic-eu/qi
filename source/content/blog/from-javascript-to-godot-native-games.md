For years my games lived in the browser. That was the point: open a tab, play, no install. In 2013 I shipped **Air Fireman** with [Impact.js](https://impactjs.com/) — a wildfire arcade where you pilot a water bomber, fight wind, and try to save the forest. It still runs today, free, in any modern browser.

Today I’m announcing something different: the **first native desktop builds** of that line of work, rebuilt with **Godot 4**. On itch.io the download is **macOS**, **Windows**, and **Linux**. On Payhip it is **macOS** and **Windows**.

## Why leave pure JavaScript?

Browser games age in a particular way. Engines and toolchains freeze; packaging for desktops becomes a maze of wrappers; performance and input feel never quite match a native executable. Impact.js gave me a complete game in 2013. Keeping that classic alive as a free web build is a feature, not a bug.

For a **remaster**, I wanted:

- A modern open engine with real export pipelines  
- Desktop controls, windowing, and installable zips  
- Room for new content without fighting a 2013 stack  

**Godot** fit that: readable scenes and scripts, solid 2D, and first-class macOS + Windows export. The free classic stays where it always was. The remaster is a separate product.
