1. Why can't you find your files? Because they sit on drives I do not plug in all at once.
1. Why is that a problem? Because I have no catalog of a drive once it is unplugged.
1. Why don't you have that catalog? Because I don't have a tool that indexes the volume and keeps the result locally.
1. Why don't you have such a tool? Because I was looking for a media library, tags, or a backup app.
1. Why that path? Because I had not seen that a disk catalog is enough to know where the file is.

## How Media Cataloger 2 answers

### Local catalog

Indexing a volume records the folder tree, modification dates, and sizes. The drive can then stay in the vault: the catalog is on the machine.

### Search

By name or extension, across catalogued drives, connected or not. No search inside document text.

### Duplicates and unique files

Copies on one disk or across several. Lists by subject, owner, or another criterion. Files that exist on only one disk. Removing an extra copy is your decision: this is not a backup tool.

### No online account

Cataloging, search, and duplicates do not need the internet. The catalog is not sent to a server.
